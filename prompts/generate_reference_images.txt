================================================================================
FeetAndShoeMeasurement — Reference Image Generation Prompts
================================================================================
Use with any image model (DALL·E, Midjourney, Flux, etc.).
Goal: produce photos the CV pipeline can measure reliably.

Shared rules (append to every prompt):
- Photorealistic smartphone photo, natural color, no illustration
- Strictly top-down / bird’s-eye view (camera parallel to the floor)
- Subject fully inside the frame; nothing cut off at edges
- Even lighting, soft shadows, NO flash glare or specular hotspots
- Dark non-white floor (wood or matte brown/gray) for contrast
- No extra objects, text overlays, watermarks, or UI chrome
- Vertical portrait framing preferred


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) A4 PAPER ONLY  (--ref paper)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Photorealistic top-down smartphone photo of a single bare adult human foot
resting on a full blank white A4 printer sheet (210×297 mm) on a dark wood floor.
The entire A4 sheet is visible with all four corners in frame. The foot is
centered on the paper, heel near one short edge, toes pointing toward the
opposite short edge, foot not hanging off the paper. Camera directly above,
orthographic-like, even daylight, no glare. High contrast between white paper,
skin, and dark floor. No shoes, no socks, no other objects.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2) CREDIT CARD ONLY  (--ref card)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Photorealistic top-down smartphone photo of a single bare adult human foot
and one standard credit card (ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1, 85.6×53.98 mm) on a dark wood
floor. The full foot (toes to heel) is visible. The blank generic white plastic
card lies flat beside the midfoot, fully visible, not overlapping the foot,
oriented portrait. No real card numbers, no logos, no name — plain white card
with a subtle chip rectangle only. Camera directly above, even lighting, no
flash glare. High contrast between card, skin, and floor. No paper, no shoes.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3) PAPER + CREDIT CARD  (--ref both)  [best test case]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Photorealistic top-down smartphone photo of a single bare adult human foot on a
full blank white A4 sheet on a dark wood floor, with one plain white credit-card-
sized plastic card lying flat on the paper next to the foot. Entire A4 sheet and
entire card visible; foot fully on the paper from heel to toes. Card does not
cover the foot. Camera directly above, even soft light, no glare. No real
branding, numbers, or logos on the card. No other objects.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4) NEGATIVE / HARD CASES (optional stress tests)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A) Paper cropped: same as (1) but bottom edge of A4 and heel cut off by frame.
B) Sideways phone: same as (2) but image stored rotated 90° (landscape pixels).
C) Flash glare: same as (2) with a bright circular flash reflection on the floor.
D) White floor: same as (2) but floor is white/light gray (poor paper/card contrast).
E) Angled shot: same as (3) but camera ~30° from vertical (perspective distortion).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5) VARIATIONS (mix into prompts 1–3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Skin tone: light / medium / dark
- Foot: left or right
- Floor: dark oak / charcoal matte / deep brown tile
- Card color: plain white / matte light gray (still high contrast vs floor)
- Mild sock-free barefoot only; keep nails and toes clearly visible


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6) SHORT MIDJOURNEY-STYLE ONE-LINERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Paper:
/imagine photorealistic top-down photo bare foot on full white A4 paper, dark wood floor, all paper corners visible, even light, no glare --ar 3:4 --style raw

Card:
/imagine photorealistic top-down photo bare foot beside plain white credit card, dark wood floor, full foot in frame, no logos no numbers, even light, no glare --ar 3:4 --style raw

Both:
/imagine photorealistic top-down photo bare foot on full A4 paper with plain white credit card beside foot on the paper, dark wood floor, even light, no glare --ar 3:4 --style raw


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7) DEPTH / LIDAR  (--ref depth)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Capture (not generative) — image models do not produce real metric depth.

iPhone (LiDAR models) / iPad Pro:
  - Use an app that exports RGB + depth (e.g. 3D Scanner App, Record3D, Depthy)
  - Export depth as meters (.npy) or 16-bit PNG in millimeters
  - Top-down view of bare foot on the floor; full foot in frame
  - Save as:  data/myfoot.jpg  +  data/myfoot_depth.npy
    or:       data/myfoot.jpg  +  data/myfoot_depth.png

Android (ToF / ARCore depth):
  - Export aligned depth the same way; values must convert to meters

Then run:
  python main.py data/myfoot.jpg --ref depth
  python main.py data/myfoot.jpg --ref depth --depth data/myfoot_depth.png --depth-scale 0.001
  python main.py data/myfoot.jpg --ref depth --fx 1200 --fy 1200   # optional intrinsics

Synthetic smoke test (no phone needed):
  python -c "from depth_measure import make_synthetic_rgbd; print(make_synthetic_rgbd())"
  python main.py data/synthetic_depth_rgb.jpg --ref depth --fx 520 --fy 520 --cx 240 --cy 320


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After generating
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Save into data/ (e.g. data/ref_paper_01.jpg, data/ref_card_01.jpg, data/ref_both_01.jpg)
Then run:
  python main.py data/ref_paper_01.jpg --ref paper
  python main.py data/ref_card_01.jpg --ref card
  python main.py data/ref_both_01.jpg --ref both
  python main.py data/myfoot.jpg --ref depth
Check output/card_detect.jpg, output/pdraw.jpg, or output/depth_detect.jpg.
